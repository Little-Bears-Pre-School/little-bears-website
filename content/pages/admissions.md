# Admissions

## Eligibility
Children are eligible to join Little Bears from the start of term after
they turn 2 years old and can remain with us until they start
primary school.

## Sessions
We run sessions Monday to Friday, 9am &ndash; 12pm and 12pm &ndash; 3pm.
Children are welcome to attend any combination of sessions, subject to
availability.  We require children to attend a minimum of 2 sessions per
week or 5 once they are entitled to funding.

## Registration
Early registration is advisable. Our supervisor is always happy to discuss
individual needs as well as organising settling in sessions for your child
once a place is confirmed.

[Contact Us](contact){: class="button"}

## Fees
For children who are not eligible for government funding, our fees are
**&pound;18.50 per 3-hour session**.

All fees are invoiced termly, in advance.


## 15 Hours Free for 3 and 4-Year-Olds

All 3 to 4-year-olds can get [15 hours of free childcare][1] per
week, starting from the term after their 3rd birthday. You do not need to apply
for this - we will handle the arrangements when you register your child with us.

## 30 Hours Free for 2, 3 and 4-Year-Olds

Eligible working parents can get [30 hours of free childcare][2], through the [Free
Childcare for Working Parents scheme][2].

* [Check if you are eligible][3]
* [Apply for an eligibility code][4]

You must provide us with your eligibility code **before the start of the term**.

Codes need to be renewed every 3 months.

## Funding for 2-Year-Olds on Additional Support

Your 2-year-old can get funded early learning if you live in England and get any
of the following benefits:

* Income support
* Income-based Jobseeker's Allowance (JSA)
* Income-related Employment and Support Allowance (ESA)
* Universal Credit - if your household income is less than £15,400 a year after tax (not including benefit payments)
* The guaranteed element of Pension Credit

A child can also get free early education and childcare if any of the following
apply:

* They’re looked after by a local council
* They have an education, health and care (EHC) plan
* They get [Disability Living Allowance][5]
* They’ve left care under a special guardianship order, child arrangements order
  or adoption order

A child can also get free early education funding if you’re a non-UK citizen who
cannot claim benefits.

See [Free early education for 2 year olds on additional forms of support][6] for more information.

If you are entitled to this funding, please [contact us](/contact) so that we
can confirm your eligibility and we will ask you to complete a [Parental
Declaration Form][7].
